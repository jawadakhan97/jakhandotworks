# Background
I'm a science nerd in the empiricist tradition of _Francis Bacon_ and an aspiring poet working his way to expressing pain & prosody like _Du Fu_ and _Rahman Baba_. I write prose to express material experience as _John Steinbeck_, _Lu Xun_, and _Takiji Kobayashi_ did, while writing essays to make _Lord Verulam_ blush with candor.

My personal experience matches that of _Ludwig Wittgenstein_ as I too famously said in my youth, to paraphrase, "I'll either be a madman or a genius." But unfortunately I've become a madman rather than finding ingenuity. I read _Gogol_ & _Xun's_ Diary of a Madman, and found that my own journal entries matched near perfectly in ethos to what they described.

Well, c'est la vie! I burnished my understanding of science with _Popper_, _Kuhn_, _Lakatos_, and _Aristotle_, in that order. I shy away from _Latour_ and others, perferring most times what I call a naive or vulgar empiricism.

I don't have much more to say, thanks for reading!
